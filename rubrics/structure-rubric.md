# Structure Rubric

Scoring criteria for evaluating structural output (L0–L3): world-building, characters, and outlines.

## Dimensions & Weights

| # | Dimension | Weight | Focus |
|---|-----------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Conflict Driving Force & Immersion | 20% | Is the story engine compelling? |
| 2 | Character Dimensionality & Differentiation | 20% | Are characters real and distinct? |
| 3 | World Consistency & Novelty | 15% | Does the world work and surprise? |
| 4 | Pacing Density | 20% | Does every chapter earn its place? |
| 5 | Hook & Suspense Design | 15% | Will readers keep turning pages? |
| 6 | Genre Innovation | 10% | Is it fresh within its category? |

## Pass Threshold

- Weighted total >= **7.0/10**
- No single dimension below **5.0/10**
- Maximum **5 iteration rounds** before human intervention

---

## 1. Conflict Driving Force & Immersion (20%)

**What to evaluate:**
- Central conflict clarity and personal stakes for protagonist
- Escalation pattern across the story arc
- Scene-level obstacles (every scene has resistance)
- Reader motivation to continue (page-turning compulsion)

**Score guide:**
| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9–10 | Conflict is urgent, personal, escalating, and surprising. Reader cannot stop. |
| 7–8 | Strong central conflict with clear escalation. Engaging throughout. |
| 5–6 | Conflict exists but lacks escalation or personal stakes in places. |
| 3–4 | Conflict is vague, external-only, or doesn't escalate meaningfully. |
| 1–2 | No clear conflict or stakes. Events happen without tension. |

---

## 2. Character Dimensionality & Differentiation (20%)

**What to evaluate:**
- Internal contradictions (desire vs. flaw, values vs. actions)
- Distinct voices and decision-making patterns
- Transformation arcs (genuine change, not just events)
- Relationship tension (allies disagree on something fundamental)
- Cast differentiation (no two characters serve the same function)

**Score guide:**
| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9–10 | Characters feel like real people with unique voices. Arcs are transformative. |
| 7–8 | Well-developed with clear differentiation. Arcs track meaningfully. |
| 5–6 | Characters functional but some feel interchangeable or lack depth. |
| 3–4 | Thin characters. Similar voices. Arcs are superficial or absent. |
| 1–2 | Cardboard cutouts. No differentiation or internal life. |

---

## 3. World Consistency & Novelty (15%)

**What to evaluate:**
- Internal rule consistency (no violations of established mechanics)
- Specificity (not generic fantasy/sci-fi wallpaper)
- World elements as conflict generators
- At least one genuinely original element
- Coherent social/political/economic logic

**Score guide:**
| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9–10 | Internally bulletproof, strikingly original, generates conflict organically. |
| 7–8 | Consistent with fresh elements. World enriches the story. |
| 5–6 | Mostly consistent. Some generic elements. Functional but not memorable. |
| 3–4 | Inconsistencies present. World feels like a backdrop, not an engine. |
| 1–2 | Contradictions. Generic setting with no original elements. |

---

## 4. Pacing Density (20%)

**What to evaluate:**
- Every chapter advances plot, character, or world (no filler)
- Tension variation across chapters (not flat)
- Setup-to-payoff ratio appropriate for genre
- Quiet moments are earned and purposeful
- Word count allocation matches scene importance

**Score guide:**
| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9–10 | Every chapter is essential. Rhythm feels intentional and dynamic. |
| 7–8 | Well-paced with appropriate variation. Minor trimming possible. |
| 5–6 | Some flat stretches or rushed sections. Uneven density. |
| 3–4 | Significant pacing issues. Filler chapters or rushed climaxes. |
| 1–2 | No sense of pacing. Random event density. |

---

## 5. Hook & Suspense Design (15%)

**What to evaluate:**
- Opening chapter creates immediate question/tension
- Micro-hooks within chapters (sentence-level pulls)
- Chapter-boundary cliffhangers or questions
- Volume-boundary hooks
- Mysteries planted before they're needed (setup precedes reveal)

**Score guide:**
| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9–10 | Masterful hook placement at every level. Impossible to stop reading. |
| 7–8 | Strong hooks at chapter level. Some effective micro-hooks. |
| 5–6 | Chapter hooks exist but are uneven. Some dead boundaries. |
| 3–4 | Hooks are weak or absent at many boundaries. No pull forward. |
| 1–2 | No hook design. Chapters end without forward momentum. |

---

## 6. Genre Innovation (10%)

**What to evaluate:**
- Meets core genre expectations (contract with reader)
- Subverts at least one genre convention meaningfully
- Distinguishable from the last 10 similar books
- Fresh combination of familiar elements
- Target audience would find it "refreshing" within the category

**Score guide:**
| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9–10 | Genre-defining potential. Honors conventions while breaking new ground. |
| 7–8 | Fresh take. Familiar enough to satisfy, novel enough to stand out. |
| 5–6 | Competent genre execution. Nothing particularly new. |
| 3–4 | Heavily derivative. Reads like recombined tropes without fresh perspective. |
| 1–2 | Pure copy of existing works. No distinguishing element. |
