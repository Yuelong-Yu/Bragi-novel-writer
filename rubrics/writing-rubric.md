# Prose Rubric

Scoring criteria for evaluating chapter prose (L4).

## Dimensions & Weights

| # | Dimension | Weight | Focus |
|---|-----------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Scene Rendering (Show vs Tell) | 15% | Are moments lived, not summarized? |
| 2 | Dialogue Naturalness & Character Voice | 15% | Do characters sound real and distinct? |
| 3 | Sensory Detail Density | 10% | Can the reader feel the scene? |
| 4 | Cinematic Visualization / 画面感 | 15% | Can the reader "see" it like a movie? |
| 5 | Pacing & Readability | 15% | Does rhythm serve content? |
| 6 | Emotional Resonance & Immersion | 12% | Does the reader feel something? |
| 7 | AI Artifact Absence | 8% | Does it read as human-written? |
| 8 | Outline Execution Completeness | 10% | Were all beats delivered? |

## Pass Threshold

- Weighted total >= **7.0/10**
- No single dimension below **5.0/10**
- Maximum **3 iteration rounds** per chapter before human intervention

---

## 1. Scene Rendering / Show vs Tell (15%)

**What to evaluate:**
- Key emotional/dramatic moments rendered as scenes (not summarized)
- Actions and sensory details convey meaning (not narrator explanation)
- Emotions demonstrated through behavior, physiology, and dialogue
- Telling reserved appropriately for transitions and low-stakes information

**Score guide:**
| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9–10 | Seamless scene rendering. Reader lives in the moment. Telling is invisible and purposeful. |
| 7–8 | Most key moments shown effectively. Occasional unnecessary telling. |
| 5–6 | Mix of showing and telling. Some key moments undercut by narrator summary. |
| 3–4 | Heavy telling. Emotions named rather than demonstrated. Report-like quality. |
| 1–2 | Pure summary. No scene rendering. Reading a plot synopsis, not a story. |

---

## 2. Dialogue Naturalness & Character Voice (15%)

**What to evaluate:**
- Character voices distinguishable without attribution
- Subtext present (characters don't say exactly what they mean)
- Natural rhythm (not all characters speak in the same cadence)
- Grounding beats between lines (action, reaction, thought)
- Attribution unobtrusive (said + beats, not said-bookisms)

**Score guide:**
| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9–10 | Could identify speakers blind. Rich subtext. Dialogue feels overheard. |
| 7–8 | Distinct voices with good subtext. Occasionally generic. |
| 5–6 | Functional dialogue. Voices somewhat similar. Limited subtext. |
| 3–4 | Generic dialogue. All characters sound alike. No subtext. |
| 1–2 | Expository dialogue. Characters explain things to each other for the reader. |

---

## 3. Sensory Detail Density (10%)

**What to evaluate:**
- At least 2 senses engaged per scene (not just visual)
- Details are specific, not generic ("the sharp tang of pine resin" not "it smelled like trees")
- Sensory details serve atmosphere and mood
- Variety in which senses are used across scenes
- Details grounded in POV character's experience

**Score guide:**
| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9–10 | Rich, varied, specific. The reader inhabits the space. Never gratuitous. |
| 7–8 | Good sensory grounding. Mostly specific. Some scenes richer than others. |
| 5–6 | Adequate but generic. Mostly visual. Some scenes feel ungrounded. |
| 3–4 | Sparse and generic. Floating-heads scenes. Reader has no physical sense of place. |
| 1–2 | No sensory detail. Pure abstraction. |

---

## 4. Cinematic Visualization / 画面感 (15%)

**What to evaluate:**
- Spatial choreography: scenes have a clear "camera" — establishing shots, mid-shots, close-ups arranged with intent
- Dynamic motion: action sequences read like storyboards, each beat composable into a visual frame
- Environment-as-mood: setting descriptions don't just list objects — lighting, weather, color temperature, and spatial depth serve the emotional register of the scene
- Visual contrast and impact: memorable images created through juxtaposition (scale contrast, light/dark, stillness/motion, beauty/horror)
- Continuity of spatial logic: the reader can mentally reconstruct character positions, movement paths, and sight lines throughout a scene
- Focal point discipline: each scene has a dominant visual image the reader's mental eye is drawn to

**Score guide:**
| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9–10 | Cinematic mastery. Every scene plays like a directed film sequence. Visual contrasts create indelible images. Spatial logic is flawless. |
| 7–8 | Strong visual quality. Most scenes have clear spatial choreography and mood-serving environments. Occasional flat compositions. |
| 5–6 | Adequate but static. Some scenes visualize well; others read like stage directions or floating dialogue. Environment is backdrop, not atmosphere. |
| 3–4 | Weak visualization. Scenes lack spatial awareness. No deliberate camera movement or visual contrast. Reader cannot picture the space. |
| 1–2 | No visual imagination. Pure abstraction or talking heads in a void. |

---

## 5. Pacing & Readability (15%)

**What to evaluate:**
- Sentence length variation (deliberate, not random)
- Paragraph length variation
- Rhythm matches content (short/punchy for action, flowing for reflection)
- No "dead zones" where attention drifts
- White space used effectively for impact

**Score guide:**
| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9–10 | Masterful rhythm control. Prose propels the reader. Impossible to skim. |
| 7–8 | Good variation. Reads smoothly with occasional momentum. |
| 5–6 | Adequate but monotonous in places. Some flat passages. |
| 3–4 | Uniform rhythm. Blocky paragraphs. Reader's eye glazes. |
| 1–2 | No rhythm awareness. Wall-of-text or choppy throughout. |

---

## 6. Emotional Resonance & Immersion (12%)

**What to evaluate:**
- Emotional beats earned through prior setup
- Reader empathy achieved (feeling WITH characters, not observing them feel)
- Emotional specificity (not generic sentimentality)
- Proportionality (big moments feel big, small moments don't overreach)
- Immersion sustained (reader forgets they're reading)

**Score guide:**
| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9–10 | Genuine emotional impact. Reader is moved. Immersion never breaks. |
| 7–8 | Solid emotional engagement. Most beats land. Minor breaks in immersion. |
| 5–6 | Some emotional connection but uneven. Reader stays at arm's length in places. |
| 3–4 | Flat emotionally. Reader observes but doesn't feel. Frequent immersion breaks. |
| 1–2 | No emotional engagement. Clinical or overwrought throughout. |

---

## 7. AI Artifact Absence (8%)

**What to evaluate — these are red flags:**
- "As a [role/title], [character] felt..." constructions
- "Little did they know..." / "In a world where..." / "It was a [adjective] day..."
- Excessive adverb-verb pairs ("said softly", "walked briskly", "nodded thoughtfully")
- Uniform paragraph length (3–4 sentences each, robotic cadence)
- Emotional labeling ("She felt sad/happy/angry") instead of demonstration
- Repetitive sentence openers (Subject-Verb-Object chains)
- Overly formal register in casual/intimate contexts
- Summarizing transitions where scene continuation is expected
- "And so..." / "Thus..." / "With that..." closure patterns
- Perfect grammar in dialogue where contractions/fragments would be natural

**Score guide:**
| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9–10 | Reads completely human. No detectable patterns. Organic and unpredictable. |
| 7–8 | Occasional minor tells but doesn't distract. Mostly organic. |
| 5–6 | Noticeable AI patterns in places. A careful reader would suspect. |
| 3–4 | Obvious AI tells throughout. Reads like polished machine output. |
| 1–2 | Unmistakably AI-generated. Template patterns everywhere. |

---

## 8. Outline Execution Completeness (10%)

**What to evaluate:**
- ALL beats from the chapter outline are present in the prose
- Key beats given appropriate weight (not rushed through)
- Chapter opening hook present and effective
- Chapter-ending hook/cliffhanger present and compelling
- No invented major plot events not in the outline
- Beat ordering follows outline logic (unless reordering clearly improves flow)

**Score guide:**
| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9–10 | All beats executed with appropriate weight. Hooks land perfectly. |
| 7–8 | All beats present. Most well-weighted. Hooks functional. |
| 5–6 | Most beats present but some rushed or underweight. Hooks weak. |
| 3–4 | Missing beats. Major events added without outline basis. Poor hooks. |
| 1–2 | Outline largely ignored. Different story than planned. |
